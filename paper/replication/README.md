## The argument in five sentences

1. The thesis's own experiments replicate (22 of 24, 2 in part) — once the
   reimplementation is faithful.
2. Its central claim, object-based inhibition of return in dynamic scenes, holds
   under a confirmatory protocol, against a strengthened baseline, on three
   blocks of fresh scenes and two parameter sets.
3. Its scanpaths are not a model of human free viewing, and were never claimed
   to be.
4. For ten weeks the project's record said (2) was false, with good-looking
   explanations; the cause was two infidelities of the port, and only the
   thesis's smallest experiments found them.
5. So: replicate the unit-level experiments before the headline claim; mind
   absolute vs relative scales; do not mistake a function's defaults for the
   deployed configuration; commit predictions, pessimistic ones included.

## Constraints

- **The original sources cannot be published or quoted** (the author does not
  hold the rights). The paper reports facts taken from them — parameter values,
  algorithmic steps — and names this as a limitation. Appendix A is the only
  window onto them: the author checks every value there by hand.
- Self-replication: said in the introduction and the limitations, not hidden.
- The reimplementation was written with an AI coding assistant; the disclosure
  is a `\todo` in §3.1 for the author to word.
